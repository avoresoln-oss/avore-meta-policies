AVORE Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Request Deletion

If you want AVORE to delete personal information associated with your interaction with our application, submit a deletion request through the contact method provided by AVORE for this application.

What to Include

Please provide the relevant Instagram username or other account identifier and, where applicable, enough information to identify the interaction or data you want deleted. Do not send passwords or other sensitive authentication credentials.

Verification

AVORE may request reasonable information to verify that the request relates to the person making the request before deleting data.

Processing

After verification, AVORE will review the request and delete or anonymize information that is not required to be retained for legitimate operational, security, or legal purposes. Data held directly by third-party platforms may need to be deleted through those platforms separately.

Contact

For a deletion request, please use the contact method provided by AVORE for this application.
