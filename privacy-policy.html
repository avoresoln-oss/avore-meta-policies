AVORE Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

AVORE operates software and automation services that may interact with social-media platforms, including Instagram, to provide requested information, resources, and automated responses.

Information We May Process

Depending on how a person interacts with our services, we may process information made available through the relevant platform, such as an Instagram account identifier, username, message content, messages sent to our automation, and information required to provide a requested resource.

How We Use Information

  • To receive and respond to messages and requests.
  • To provide requested documents, links, or other resources.
  • To operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the automation service.
  • To prevent abuse, spam, fraud, and unauthorized use.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the automation, such as hosting, workflow automation, storage, and platform services. Such processing is limited to the purposes required to provide and maintain the service.

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including providing the requested service, maintaining security, meeting operational requirements, or complying with applicable legal obligations.

Third-Party Platforms

Interactions through Instagram and other Meta services are also subject to the applicable Meta and Instagram terms and privacy policies. AVORE does not control the privacy practices of those third-party platforms.

Data Deletion and Privacy Requests

You may request deletion of personal information processed by AVORE by contacting AVORE through the contact details associated with this application. Please include enough information to identify the relevant account or interaction. We may need to verify the request before processing it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

Contact

For privacy or data-deletion requests, please use the contact method provided by AVORE for this application.
